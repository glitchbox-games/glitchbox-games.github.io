IMPOSTER

everyone knows the word · except one

HOW TO PLAY

  • Everyone secretly sees the same word, like PIZZA. Everyone except one person, who only sees 🕵️ YOU'RE THE IMPOSTER.
  • Go round the circle. Each person says one word about the secret word: "cheesy", "slice"…
  • Be clever. Too obvious and the imposter works out the word. Too vague and people think you're the imposter!
  • The imposter has to bluff a clue that fits in.
  • Then everyone votes for who they think the imposter is.
  • Catch them and the crew wins… unless the imposter can guess the word to steal the win.
  • Points: crew +1 each for a catch (+1 more for voting right). The imposter gets +2 for getting away or guessing the word.
  • Sneaky mode: the imposter gets a different word and doesn't know they're the imposter. Chaos.
