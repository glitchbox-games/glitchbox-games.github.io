FIRST FIGHT?

Every punch here has an answer: slip the straight, duck the hook, block the rest. The walkthrough on the gym dummy teaches one at a time, then how to chain punches on the beat. Stop whenever you like. Or skip it and fight a ROOKIE CPU: moving, jab, cross and block are enough to start.

HOW TO PLAY

Every punch has a type, and every type has an answer. Read what's coming, answer it, hit back.

READING IT

As a fighter winds up, a stamp over them shows what's coming. Hooks and uppercuts are slow enough to answer on reaction. Straights aren't, so those you guess. You can switch the stamps off in SETTINGS once you can read the wind-ups.

THE BEAT

Press the next punch as the last one lands and it chains with half the wind-up: no gap to slip into. Press too early and it waits out the whole recovery instead, leaving you open. Mashing never chains. Hands alternate, left-right-left (a double jab is fine), up to four in a row.

Land a named string on the beat for a bonus. Boxers count punches: 1 jab, 2 cross, 3 hook, 6 uppercut. Try 1-2, 1-2-3, 2-3-2, 1-6-3, 3-6, 1-2-3-2, or a body hook into a head hook.

CONTROLS

W A S DMove, relative to the screen. You always face your opponent. On the broadcast camera D steps in and A backs off. J K L IJab, cross, hook, uppercut. Mouse left jabs, mouse right crosses. THE FIGHT

Three rounds of a minute. Drop to zero and you're down: mash punch to beat the count. Three knockdowns is a TKO. Still standing at the end? The judges score each round.

Punches cost stamina, and a whiff costs the same as a hit. Gas out and you're slow, weak and easy to clinch.

RULES on the select screen: SUPER ABILITIES, or PURE BOXING with every ability and passive switched off.
