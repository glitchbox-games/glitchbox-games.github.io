HOW TO PLAY

  • A question pops up: "Who's most likely to… cry at a sad film?"
  • Everyone votes for the person in the group who fits best. You can vote for yourself if you're honest!
  • The votes are revealed and whoever gets the most is crowned 👑.
  • Vote for the winner and you score a 🧠 mind-reader point for knowing your group.
  • At the end everyone gets their awards: every crown they won.
  • Add your own questions in the lobby, or have everyone write some on their phones. They're the best ones.
  • On one screen? Count down 3-2-1 and everyone points, or pass the phone round for secret votes.
