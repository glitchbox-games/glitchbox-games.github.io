SCRAWL

one draws · everyone guesses · 2-8 players

HOW TO PLAY

  • Make a room and share the 4-letter code (or invite friends from the lobby).
  • Each turn one player picks one of three words and draws it. No letters or numbers allowed!
  • Everyone else types guesses in the chat. The faster you guess, the more points you get.
  • Letters get revealed as hints while the clock runs down. Almost there? You'll see "is close!".
  • Once you've guessed it, your messages only go to others who guessed it too — no spoilers.
  • The drawer scores for every player who gets it. Most points after the last round wins.
