HOW TO PLAY

  • A word shows up, like CAT. Whoever holds the 💣 has to type a word that rhymes with it: hat, splat, acrobat…
  • A good rhyme passes the bomb to the next player. A word that doesn't rhyme, or that someone already said, gets rejected. Try again fast.
  • The fuse gets shorter with every rhyme. If it runs out while you hold the bomb, BOOM: you lose a ❤️ and a new word comes up.
  • Run out of hearts and you're out. The last rhymer standing wins.
  • You score a point per syllable for every rhyme you land, so diplomat beats bat.
  • Hot potato mode: the bomb has a secret timer. Nobody knows when it'll blow.
