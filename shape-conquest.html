Shape Conquest

Pick any country on Earth. Build an army of shapes. Take over the world one capital at a time.

Every country starts free and every one of them wants more land. To take a country, smash its capital's defences, then park a ground unit on it.

⛔ Supply: troops more than a short march from your land are out of supply — they wither and fight weaker, so push in fronts and use warships to carry supply overseas. Troops heal on home soil, defenders fight harder there, railways move troops 60% faster across your own land, new conquests take 90s to integrate before they pay in full or raise troops, and veterans (★ from kills) hit harder — retreat (H) a beaten army instead of throwing it away.

🕊 Diplomacy: you start at peace with everyone. Your troops only move when you order them, and they can't cross a border or attack until you declare war — an unprovoked war turns the victim's neighbours against you. Make peace when you're winning (or pay for it when you're not). Warships need a ⚓ dock.

🧠 Economy: real powers (★) start big and rich. Tanks, fighters and warships burn ⛽ fuel — only oil countries supply it. Every unit costs upkeep, so balance army vs 🏭 factories and 🔬 research. Sign 🤝 pacts to guard a flank, then 📣 call those allies into your wars, or park an army on a small country and demand its surrender. Click any country for options.

⚠ The world fights back. Coalitions of neighbours launch invasion waves at you, every conquest earns you grudges, and big empires breed rebels. Fortify your borders and keep missiles ready.
